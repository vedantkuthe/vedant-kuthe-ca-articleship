06 / What happened in the banking sector?

Selected material developments during the review window. Q2 FY27 results for several banks were released on 17 October 2026.
17 OCT · RESULTS

Profit growth diverged

Reported Q2 FY27 standalone net profit rose 4.7% at HDFC Bank, 20.1% at ICICI Bank and 38% at Axis Bank year-on-year. This showed different earnings momentum across the peer group.

17 OCT · MARGINS

Margin pressure persisted

Net interest margins were lower sequentially for HDFC, ICICI and Axis in Q2 FY27. Deposit pricing and funding mix remained important watch-points despite loan growth.

Q2 FY27 · SECTOR

IndusInd recovery remained a watch item

Sector coverage noted IndusInd returned to profitability, while stressed assets remained elevated. A recovery thesis therefore still carried material execution and asset-quality risk.

These are company/sector developments observed during the period. They are not asserted as the sole cause of any individual share-price move.

08 / What could go wrong?

  • Deposit growth lagging advances could increase funding costs.
  • Higher slippages, credit costs or unsecured-loan stress could erode earnings.
  • Net interest margins may compress if deposit rates rise faster than lending yields.
  • Regulatory, governance, cyber or operational incidents can damage confidence.
  • Valuation multiples may contract even when profits grow.
  • All five holdings are banks, so sector concentration creates correlated downside risk.
The biggest portfolio lesson: a strong business thesis does not remove entry-price risk. Diversification across five banks is not the same as diversification across sectors.
